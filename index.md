# Radio Silence

A cooperative party game for two to six people. You share a deck of cards
numbered 1 to 100, and you have to play them all in ascending order — without
saying a word, without signalling, and without any turn order. Play a card
while someone else still holds a lower one and the team takes the hit.

Each level deals everyone one more card than the last. Clearing the final one
completes the constellation, and that is the ending.
