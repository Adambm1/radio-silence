# Radio Silence

A cooperative party game for two to six people. You share a deck of cards
numbered 1 to 100, and you have to play them all in ascending order — without
saying a word, without so much as a look, and without any turn order. Play a
card while someone else still holds a lower one and the team takes the hit.

Each level deals everyone one more card than the last, and between them the
radio is still breaking up. Clear the final one and the static loses: the
signal comes through, and for the only time in the game somebody speaks.
